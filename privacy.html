GlassHouse · privacy

For the GlassHouse app for iPhone and iPad, and the web version at linglab.org/glasshouse. Last updated November 26, 2026.

The short version

GlassHouse collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The physics, the sound and the visuals are all computed on your own device.

Touch and sound

GlassHouse uses your touches only to play the instrument as you touch it. It does not use the microphone, and it never records, saves or sends anything you play.

MIDI and Bluetooth

If you connect a MIDI keyboard or controller, the app reads its notes and controller messages to play the glasses, and shows the names of connected devices so you can see what it is listening to. Bluetooth is used only when you open the Bluetooth MIDI panel and choose to connect a device. Nothing about your devices leaves your iPhone or iPad.

Settings

Choices like the glass shape or the reverb are kept only while the app is open. Nothing is stored in an online account, because there isn't one.

The app makes no network connections

Everything the app needs, including its typefaces, is inside the app. It works in airplane mode.

The web version

The web version is a set of static files served by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else. As with any website, the host (GitHub) may log the IP address of visitors; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

GlassHouse collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions are welcome on the support page.
